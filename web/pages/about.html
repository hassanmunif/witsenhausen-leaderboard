About

This site is maintained by Hassan Munif, CRAN (UMR 7039), CNRS, Université de Lorraine. It brings together what is known about Witsenhausen’s counterexample: the best controllers found so far, with their costs recomputed by one open evaluator wherever a controller file exists; the proven lower bounds; and results about the optimum.

Contact and corrections

If an entry about your work is wrong or incomplete, or a result is missing, write to hassan.munif0@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub. Controller files can be sent by email too: a GitHub account is not needed.

What is listed

  • Costs printed in papers or preprints for one of the settings on the leaderboard, as printed. They are marked claimed until a controller file reproduces them.
  • Controller files from anyone. Each is scored by the evaluator of Section 3 and ranked by its verified cost. An entry made with the help of an AI model says which model and what it did, and is tagged AI-assisted.
  • Lower bounds whose proof is published, computed numerically and rounded down.
  • Results about the optimum itself, marked peer-reviewed or preprint (Section 5).

Disputed claims

A claimed cost is marked disputed only when there is evidence that the printed number is not the cost of the paper’s controller, and that evidence is published in the repository. When a claim is marked disputed, its authors will be contacted and invited to reply, and a reply will be linked from the entry. The mark is removed if a controller file reproduces the printed cost.

Conflict of interest

The record at every setting is currently held by controllers from this site’s own reference optimizer, run by the maintainer. They are scored by the same evaluator as every other entry, and their files are public. Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) suggested the curved steps and wrote the optimizer, so these entries are tagged AI-assisted.
