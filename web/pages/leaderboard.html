2Leaderboard

Controllers are ranked by cost, lower is better, and lower bounds by value, higher is better: no controller can cost less than a proven lower bound. Each entry has an evidence level, which opens the evidence behind it, and a click on a row shows its details:

  • Numerically verified: recomputed here from a controller file (Section 3).
  • Claimed: printed in a paper, not checked.
  • Peer-reviewed: a bound with a published proof, computed here and rounded down.
Disputed claims, records, AI assistance, ties and dates

A claimed cost is marked disputed when there is evidence that the printed number is not the cost of the paper’s controller. Disputed claims are hidden unless you tick Disputed. They are not ranked, and their details give the reason.

An entry tagged AI-assisted was found with the help of an AI model, as its authors declare. Its tooltip and its details say which model and what it did.

The verified entry marked record holds the record under the record rule: it was the first to reach its cost. Verified costs closer together than that rule can separate share a rank, shown as =1, with the record holder listed first. Ranks count every verified entry and every claim that is not disputed, so they stay the same when you untick an evidence level.

Costs are shown to nine decimals at most; the full value is in the tooltip and in the details. A controller taken from a paper is dated by the paper’s year; its details name who made the controller file and when it was verified here.
