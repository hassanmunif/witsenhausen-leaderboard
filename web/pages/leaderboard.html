2Leaderboard

Controllers are ranked by cost, lower is better, and lower bounds by value, higher is better: no controller can cost less than a proven lower bound. Each entry has an evidence level:

  • Numerically verified: recomputed here from a controller file (Section 3).
  • Claimed: printed in a paper, not checked.
  • Peer-reviewed: a bound with a published proof, computed here and rounded down.
Disputed claims, records, ties and dates

A claimed cost is marked disputed when there is evidence that the printed number is not the cost of the paper’s controller. Disputed claims are hidden unless you tick Disputed, and their details give the reason.

The verified entry marked record holds the record under the record rule: it was the first to reach its cost. Verified costs closer together than that rule can separate share a rank, shown as =1, with the record holder listed first. A controller taken from a paper is dated by the paper’s year; its details name who made the controller file and when it was verified here.
