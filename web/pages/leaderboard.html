2Leaderboard

Controllers are ranked by their cost: lower is better. Lower bounds are ranked by value: higher is better, because no controller can cost less than a proven lower bound. Each entry has an evidence level.

  • Numerically verified: this site recomputed the cost from the submitted controller file (Section 3).
  • Peer-reviewed: the proof of the bound was published in a peer-reviewed paper.
  • Claimed: stated but not checked, such as a cost printed in a paper whose controller has not been submitted.
